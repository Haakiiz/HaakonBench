# The One-Spot Fishing Business
### For a level 60 Human Warrior · Alliance · Fishing 300 · Cooking 300

**Your best fishing upgrade is an instance portal.**

I would build this character’s operation around **Deviate Fish at Dreamer’s Rock inside Wailing Caverns**, then use Cooking to profit from other people’s catches when the Deviate market is weak.

Not an Azshara circuit. Not a guarded dock with an escape macro. Not a shoreline that is safe until its patrol returns.

Your scarce resource is not fishing time. It is **attention you can afford to divert from staying alive**.

This guide assumes **Classic Era’s original 1–300 professions, ordinary trading, and a PvE realm**. It does not import Season of Discovery or expansion recipes.

> **One necessary distinction:** AFK-safe fishing is not automated fishing. You still cast and collect each catch yourself. When you stop interacting, production stops; the fishing system does not keep harvesting for you. The goal is a station where missing a bite costs a fish—not your character. If you cannot interact at all, your fishing income is **0g/hour**. ([warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/fishing/?utm_source=openai))

## 1. The station: beside the sleeping druid

### Approved: Dreamer’s Rock, inside your own Wailing Caverns instance

Specifically: **the dry fishing position beside sleeping Naralex, overlooking the water in his chamber**.

This is not:

- The Barrens oasis outside the cave.
- The underground lake **before** the instance portal.
- An arbitrary bank beside the first crocolisks.
- A patch of ground made temporarily empty by killing its occupants.

Deviate Fish are catchable inside the instance without schools. The Naralex/Mutanus chamber is specifically documented as a fishing location that avoids the ordinary respawn problem; contemporary item comments also identify the sleeping druid’s location as having no mobs. ([warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/deviate-fish/?utm_source=openai))

### Why this position passes—and “near some guards” does not

| Threat | What removes it |
|---|---|
| **Other players attacking or bringing trouble to you** | Enter solo and remain solo. Your fishing session is inside your own dungeon instance, not in the public entrance cave. |
| **Ambient patrols and respawns** | Use Naralex’s fishing position, not the approach corridor or ordinary dungeon shoreline. Safety comes from the position, not a recently cleared pack. |
| **The chamber’s encounter spawns** | **Do not start the Disciple of Naralex escort.** Its awakening sequence summons enemies, including Mutanus. |
| **Drowning** | Stand on dry ground. Do not fish while depending on a swimming position or temporary effect. |
| **An interruption becoming a combat emergency** | Once parked correctly, no ongoing defensive action is part of the plan. |

The private-instance advantage and the special suitability of the final chamber are documented separately from the dungeon’s dangerous entrance waters. The awakening encounter is an explicitly triggered event, not something that spontaneously starts because you stand near Naralex. ([warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/deviate-fish/?utm_source=openai))

**Leave the rest of the dungeon alone.** You do not need to finish Wailing Caverns. Leaving Fanglords alive also leaves the awakening event’s prerequisites unfinished. The original dungeon walkthrough distinguishes the route to Naralex from the longer boss circuit and explains the escort’s triggered waves. ([ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/imprint_downloads/brady/wow/wailing/wailinglr.pdf))

### The safety rules are deliberately boring

1. **No party invitations during the session.** Privacy is part of the safety mechanism.
2. **No escort event.**
3. **No repositioning toward the corridor to improve a cast.**
4. **After a disconnect, reset, or new login, inspect your actual location before resuming.** Yesterday’s setup is not a substitute for today’s.
5. **Use your Hearthstone for the planned departure.** Do not make the return journey through the dungeon part of an inattentive fishing session.

An escape route is useful for leaving. It is not an excuse to approve a position that eventually requires escape.

### Why I am not offering an outdoor fallback

Public-world safety depends on more than the local spawn map. Blizzard’s own Hardcore rules discuss dangerous creatures being dragged into otherwise low-level areas and cities; they mitigate that behavior rather than establish universal invulnerability. ([wow-site-bwa-production-eks-prod-use1-01.worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com](https://wow-site-bwa-production-eks-prod-use1-01.worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23973734/rules-of-engagement-classic-hardcore-is-coming-to-world-of-warcraft?utm_source=openai))

Under your constraint, “usually undisturbed” is not a second-best recommendation. It is a rejected recommendation.

**If this station is unavailable, the fallback is not a more dangerous fish. It is a different business activity—or logging out.**

## 2. The route: one expedition, not a shoreline circuit

### Before departure: make the selling decision

At the Alliance auction house, record **per-fish**, not per-stack, prices for:

- Deviate Fish
- Savory Deviate Delight
- Raw Nightfin Snapper
- Nightfin Soup
- Winter Squid
- Grilled Squid

These are your proposed sale prices, not proof that buyers will purchase unlimited quantities. Initially, test a small quantity rather than valuing an entire afternoon’s production at the cheapest visible listing.

Buy or learn the relevant recipes before leaving. **Cooking 300 does not mean you know every recipe.** Savory Deviate Delight requires only Cooking 85 but its recipe is a Barrens drop; Nightfin Soup and Grilled Squid recipes are sold by Gikkix at Steamwheedle Port, Tanaris. For this character, purchasing a reasonably priced recipe from another player can be preferable to adding a dedicated acquisition trip. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

### Outbound route

**Alliance city → Booty Bay by an already unlocked flight route → boat to Ratchet → west toward Wailing Caverns → through the actual instance portal.**

The Booty Bay–Ratchet boat is the straightforward Alliance connection. The cave entrance is approximately **46, 37 in the Barrens**. Stay away from Horde settlements rather than following roads through them. ([warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Barrens_%28Classic%29?utm_source=openai))

Inside:

**Entrance → main cavern/Screaming Gully → branch toward Naralex’s chamber → dry position beside the sleeper.**

Use the sleeper as the landmark, not a guessed dungeon coordinate. The original map marks Naralex on the short northern branch off the main cavern; you do not need the long eastern boss loop. ([ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/imprint_downloads/brady/wow/wailing/wailinglr.pdf))

**Travel and positioning are attended operations.** Keep your combat equipment equipped until you are parked. If you cannot give the setup your attention, do not begin it. On a PvP realm, I would not certify the overland journey as a no-death route.

### What to bring

- Your existing fishing pole.
- Plenty of empty bag space.
- Mild Spices if cooking your catch.
- Flint and Tinder plus Simple Wood if you want to cook at the station.
- Your Hearthstone, bound to your chosen Alliance business hub.

Basic Campfire uses Simple Wood and requires Flint and Tinder; it lets you cook without leaving your position. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell%3D818/basic-campfire?utm_source=openai))

**Do not buy fishing upgrades for this operation.** At Fishing 300, you are already far above the skill needed for Deviate fishing. A more prestigious pole does not solve a problem this station has. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

### Return in batches

Fish, cook the quantity you intend to sell cooked, then hearth when you can attend the selling and resupply work.

Do not repeatedly interrupt a long session to sell one stack. Conversely, do not throw away every incidental catch just to avoid ever returning. Bag handling and selling are logistics; schedule them instead of improvising them while distracted.

## 3. Cooking: the fish you caught is not free

The most expensive mistake in fishing guides is treating cooked sale revenue as cooking profit.

There are **two different calculations**.

Let:

- \(R\) = realistic raw-fish sale price
- \(C\) = realistic cooked-item sale price
- \(S\) = additional ingredient cost

The faction auction house takes **5%** of a successful sale. A successful auction returns its deposit; failed listings can still cost you money. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/classic-auction-house-economy?utm_source=openai))

### If you caught the fish

Compare cooking with selling that same fish raw:

\[
\boxed{\text{Extra value from cooking}=0.95(C-R)-S}
\]

### If you bought the fish

Your purchase does not receive an auction-house discount:

\[
\boxed{\text{Trading margin}=0.95C-R-S}
\]

Those formulas are before failed deposits, recipe acquisition and your selling overhead.

### Three recipes worth understanding properly

| Product | Exact conversion | What you are selling |
|---|---|---|
| **Savory Deviate Delight** | 1 Deviate Fish + 1 Mild Spices → 1 Delight | A cosmetic transformation consumable. Your core product. |
| **Nightfin Soup** | 1 Raw Nightfin Snapper + 1 Refreshing Spring Water → 1 Soup | **8 mana per 5 seconds for 10 minutes.** A separate customer base from Deviates. |
| **Grilled Squid** | 1 Winter Squid + 1 Soothing Spices → 1 Grilled Squid | **10 Agility for 10 minutes**, after eating long enough to obtain the buff. |

The conversions are one-for-one, not multiple cooked servings per fish. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell%3D8238/savory-deviate-delight))

### Worked prices—not invented market quotations

The following auction prices are **illustrative inputs**, not claims about your realm:

- Deviate Fish **20s**; Delight **30s**
- Raw Nightfin **15s**; Soup **25s**
- Winter Squid **40s**; Grilled Squid **41s**

For ingredients, use undiscounted vendor costs of **2c per Mild Spices**, **5c per Refreshing Spring Water**, and **1s60c per Soothing Spices**. Mild Spices and water are sold in bundles; do not mistake the bundle price for the per-item cost. Reputation discounts can improve these slightly. ([warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Mild_Spices?utm_source=openai))

| Recipe | Additional value per fish you caught | Margin per fish bought at the stated raw price | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Savory Deviate Delight | **9s48c** | **8s48c** | Cook, provided the cooked stock sells |
| Nightfin Soup | **9s45c** | **8s70c** | Attractive small-batch conversion |
| Grilled Squid | **−65c** | **−2s65c** | **Do not cook** |

That gives:

- **100 self-caught Deviates:** cooking adds **9g48s** over selling them raw.
- **100 purchased Nightfin:** conversion earns **8g70s** before overhead.
- **100 purchased Winter Squid:** the apparently higher cooked price produces a **2g65s loss**.

**A higher cooked price is not sufficient.** It must clear both the ingredient cost and the alternative value of the raw fish.

### Recipe payback: charge it to the right income

Suppose the Deviate recipe costs **30g**, and your extra value from cooking remains **9s48c per fish**.

It takes **317 cooked sales** to repay that recipe **relative to selling the fish raw**.

Do not claim that the recipe paid for itself after selling 30g worth of Delights. Most of that revenue was already present in the fish.

### Where Cooking 300 does *not* help

Ordinary leveling fish have another customer: people leveling Cooking. For example, Raw Redgill feeds the Cooking 225 stage. Turning every raw fish into ordinary food can remove that customer without creating a better one. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

My rule:

**Cook a sale, not a bag.**

Keep raw inventory until you know which form deserves production. Your profession rank gives you options; it does not obligate you to process everything.

## 4. Honest gold per hour

There is no defensible universal “Deviate fishing makes 25g/hour” claim without your realm’s prices, your actual collection rate and your ability to sell the output.

But there is a useful model.

### Working assumptions

For this example:

- **30% of landed catches are Deviate Fish.**
- Delights sell for the illustrative **30s each**.
- Mild Spices cost **2c each**.
- Every Delight eventually sells.
- We ignore incidental catches.
- We initially exclude travel, cooking time, recipe cost and failed auctions.

The 30% is a **planning assumption**, not a guaranteed Classic drop rate. A published historical Dreamer’s Rock fishing log reported 29.8% over 2,417 successful catches; that supports the scale of the assumption, not a promise that your next session will match it. Replace it with your own station log. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/item%3D6522/deviate-fish?utm_source=openai))

Each sold Delight returns:

\[
0.30g \times 0.95 - 0.0002g = 0.2848g
\]

| Landed catches per elapsed fishing hour | Expected Deviates at 30% | Raw sales at 20s, after AH cut | Cooked sales at 30s, after AH cut and spices |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 60 | 18 | 3.42g | **5.13g** |
| 120 | 36 | 6.84g | **10.25g** |
| 180 | 54 | 10.26g | **15.38g** |

**These are scenarios, not advertised performance.** Count pauses and missed bites when measuring your landed catches per hour. Do not measure ten highly attentive minutes and extrapolate them across a distracted afternoon.

### Turn the headline into an actual session

Under the 180-catch scenario:

- Four fishing hours produce an expected **216 Deviates**.
- Their cooked sales return **61.52g** after auction cut and spices.
- At three seconds per craft, cooking takes **10.8 minutes**.
- Allow another **30 minutes**, illustratively, for travel, setup and selling.

The resulting rate is approximately **13.14g per total operating hour**, before recipe cost, failed deposits and other small expenses—not 15.38g. The three-second cooking time comes from the recipe itself. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell%3D8238/savory-deviate-delight))

### What breaks the estimate

- You collect fewer catches because real life needs you.
- Your Deviate percentage differs.
- The cooked price falls.
- Buyers absorb only a fraction of your production.
- You spend more time managing inventory and auctions than you recorded.

The last two are particularly important here.

**Unlimited fishing time does not create unlimited customers. Unsold stacks are inventory, not realized gold.**

## 5. Your routing decisions happen at the auction house

The point of choosing an instanced station is that another fisherman cannot consume your private open water. There are no schools here to chase, and no public shoreline competition to displace you. ([warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/deviate-fish/?utm_source=openai))

Your practical decision tree is therefore:

### Deviates are selling, and cooking has a worthwhile premium
**Stay with the station. Cook the volume that actually sells.**

### Raw Deviates sell better after costs
**Stay with the station. Sell raw.**

A weak cooking margin is not a reason to abandon a safe fishery.

### Delights are piling up
**Stop adding cooked inventory.**

My operating policy would be to hold no more than roughly two selling cycles’ worth of finished stock until demand is established. That is an inventory rule, not a claim about the game’s market mechanics.

### Deviate demand is poor, but purchased Nightfin has a proven spread
**Use Cooking instead of taking the Warrior to a Nightfin lake.**

Buy a small batch, cook it, sell it, and repeat only after it moves. Keep this profit separate from fishing gold/hour.

### Neither market clears your minimum worthwhile return
**Stop producing for now.**

That is the correct route. You have unlimited time, not an obligation to turn every available hour into another stack.

### A supplier’s clock, not your travel schedule

Nightfin catches favor **midnight–6 a.m. server time** and are absent **noon–6 p.m.** That can inform when you look for incoming supply; it does not prove that fish will be cheaper then. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13759/raw-nightfin-snapper?utm_source=openai))

Winter Squid are seasonal, approximately **late September through late March**. Do not interpret “September” as “available from September 1,” or assume the end of the catching season guarantees profitable speculation. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13755/winter-squid?utm_source=openai))

Your role is to buy an observed spread, not a story about a future shortage.

## 6. The quiet ways this character loses

### “I cleared the bank, so it’s safe.”

You established a temporary absence of enemies. You did not establish an AFK-safe position.

### “I’m level 60 in plate.”

That helps you handle an attended approach. It does not make an unattended hostile encounter acceptable.

### “Schools have a better Deviate percentage.”

True—but following schools changes the job into movement, positioning and public-world exposure. A better catch percentage is not automatically better performance under your constraints. ([warcrafttavern.com](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/deviate-fish/?utm_source=openai))

### “The recipe is rare, so I should farm it.”

Not for this operation. Evaluate buying it against its incremental profit. Recipe hunting is a different activity.

### “I need a better pole.”

Not here. Spend first on removing logistical friction, not skill you do not need. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

### “All these fish are worth the current auction price.”

Only the quantity that sells at that price is.

### “If I hear combat, I’ll switch weapons.”

Then the spot failed the assignment before the first cast.

## The card to keep beside the keyboard

**Before leaving**
- Check raw and cooked prices per item.
- Confirm you know the recipe.
- Empty bags; bring supplies.
- Attend the journey.

**Before relaxing**
- Inside your own Wailing Caverns instance.
- At sleeping Naralex’s dry fishing position.
- Solo.
- No awakening escort.
- No dependence on a recently killed spawn.

**While fishing**
- Collect what your attention permits.
- Missing bites is acceptable.
- Changing position or joining a group ends the unattended-safe setup.

**Before selling**
- Compare cooked proceeds with raw proceeds.
- Include costs and actual elapsed time.
- Produce to demand, not to theoretical inventory value.

**The claim I would stake this guide on:** for this Warrior, **private Deviate fishing is more useful than a richer public catch table**, and Cooking is most powerful when it lets you profit from fish you never personally risked traveling to catch.

The best session is not the one with the most valuable screenshot.

It is the one you can repeat without ever needing a rescue.
