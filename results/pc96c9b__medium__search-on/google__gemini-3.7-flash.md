### The Multiplier Recipes

#### Recipe: Grilled Squid (Requires Cooking 275)
* **Reagents**: 1x Raw Winter Squid, 1x Soothing Spices (Cost: 32c from vendor).
* **Consumer**: DPS Warriors, Rogues, Feral Druids, Hunters.
* **Economic Fact**: +10 Agility is equivalent to crit scaling for physical damage classes. It has zero alternatives until Phase 5/6. Raw Squid sells at an unrefined discount because buyers need the buff right before entering the raid.

#### Recipe: Nightfin Soup (Requires Cooking 250)
* **Reagents**: 1x Raw Nightfin Snapper, 1x Refreshing Spring Water (Cost: 24c from vendor).
* **Consumer**: Holy Paladins, Priests, Resto Druids, Mages.
* **Economic Fact**: +8 Mp5 is the single best sustained mana-per-second food in the game. It is consumed on cooldown across every tier of Classic progression.

#### Vendor Salvage: Redgill & Sunscale
* **Do not list these on the AH**. The deposit fee eats your margin if they expire.
* Buy cheap spices, craft **Filet of Redgill** / **Poached Sunscale Salmon**, and sell them directly to the vendor for a flat 100% price lift over raw vendor value.

## 5. The Contrarian Play

### Feathermoon Pier (Feralas) Over Azshara (Bay of Storms)

Most fishing guides tout **Bay of Storms (Azshara)** as the top fishing zone in Classic due to the chance of hooking Elemental Water pools and high-level lockboxes. 

**For an unattended AFK session, Azshara is a poor choice:**

```
                    BAY OF STORMS (Azshara) vs. FEATHERMOON PIER (Feralas)
+-------------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Attribute               | Bay of Storms (Azshara)          | Feathermoon Pier (Feralas)       |

| PvP Gank Vulnerability  | Fatal (High Horde transit zone)  | Immune (Lvl 60 Elite Sentinels)  |
| "Fish Got Away" Rate    | High (Skill req: 425+)           | Low (Skill req: 375-400)         |
| Pool Hunting Required   | Yes (To justify being there)     | No (Consistent open-water loot)  |

| True AFK Capability     | 0% (Fatal within 20 mins)        | 100% (Unbroken 8+ hour sessions) |
+-------------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
```

### Why Feathermoon Wins:
1. **Loot Table Uniformity**: Feathermoon open-water contains the exact same high-tier ocean table entries as Azshara (**Winter Squid, Stonescale Eel, Glossy Bay Shark**), without requiring you to move between pools.
2. **True Uptime**: In Azshara, an unexpected death 20 minutes into an 8-hour AFK session yields **7 hours and 40 minutes of dead time**. At Feathermoon, your catch continues uninterrupted for the full session duration.
3. **Turnover Speed**: The mailbox and vendor sit on the same island. You can empty bags, cook, post to the Auctioneer alt via mailbox, and get back to casting in under two minutes.

### 1. The Hidden Skill Deficit (The "Got Away" Tax)
* **The Trap**: Fishing in Moonglade (Lvl 400 cap) or Feralas (Lvl 400-425 cap) with raw 300 Fishing skill and no lure.
* **The Cost**: Every time the game displays *"Your fish got away"*, you waste a full 20-second channel without rolling on the loot table. At a 20% miss rate, you lose **24 casts per hour**.
* **The Fix**: 
  * Keep **Nat Pagle’s Extreme Angler FC-5000** (+25) or **Big Iron Fishing Pole** (+20).
  * Equip **Enchanted Gloves: +2 Fishing**.
  * Keep a full 200-stack of **Bright Baubles (+75)** or **Aquadynamic Fish Attractors (+100)** in your bags. Reapply on a strict rotation.

### 2. The Sunday Listing Dump (The AEC Crash)
* **The Trap**: Emptying your bags on Sunday evening and dumping 25 stacks of Nightfin Soup and Grilled Squid onto the AH at 24-hour durations.
* **The Cost**: Sunday night has high supply (casual weekend gatherers dumping loot) and low raid demand. Your auctions will undercut the market downward, and competitors will post on top of you. You pay deposit fees, fail to sell, and push the price down ahead of the mid-week reset.
* **The Fix**: 
  * Mail raw/cooked stacks to a dedicated **Bank/AH Alt** throughout the week.
  * Hold inventory in your mailbox until **Tuesday at 15:00 Server Time** and **Thursday at 16:00 Server Time**.
  * Post in stacks of **20 (Full Raid Consumable Set)** or **10**, never singles. Set duration to **8 Hours** during prime raid windows (17:00 – 23:00) to minimize undercut exposure and maximize turnover.

### 3. Inventory Bag Choking
* **The Trap**: Leaving open inventory slots vulnerable to low-value junk fish (Raw Spotted Yellowtail, Raw Mithril Head Trout, Grey trash items).
* **The Cost**: Your 60-slot free space fills up within 2.5 hours of a long AFK session. For the remaining 5.5 hours, high-value fish (Squid, Nightfin, Stonescale) are lost to full-inventory errors.
* **The Fix**: 
  * Setup your bags intentionally: carry maximum-capacity containers (e.g., Runecloth or Traveler's Backpacks).
  * Set auto-loot rules or organize bags so low-value fish are automatically processed into vendor food, sold to nearby dock merchants, or cleared before each long session.
