## Bottom line

For a level 60 Human Warrior with **Fishing 300 / Cooking 300** and a true **AFK-only** constraint, the best money is **not** Bay of Storms. Your real route is:

**Tanaris as the baseline**, **Winterspring/Jademir Lake for daytime Whitescale**, **Moonglade or The Hinterlands for night fishing**, and **cooking only the fish that clears a real vendor/AH premium**. As of late July, **Winter Squid is out of season**, so don’t build your plan around it right now; **Raw Summer Bass** is the active seasonal bass until the fall equinox. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

## The route, not the spot list

### 1) **Default workhorse: Tanaris — Steamwheedle Port**
**Use this when you want one place you can park for hours.** Wowhead explicitly calls Steamwheedle Port a **quiet, safe spot** and says you can stay there all the way to 300. It yields **Raw Spotted Yellowtail**, **Firefin Snapper**, **Raw Glossy Mightfish**, and, by season, **Raw Summer Bass** or **Winter Squid**. The surrounding coast is mostly **neutral turtles**, and the only real risk is drifting too far south into hostile Wastewander territory. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/fishing-leveling-1-300-wow-classic))

**Gold/hour estimate:** **8–12g/h vendor floor**, **15–25g/h if your cooked stacks actually move on the AH**. This collapses if your server has cheap raw fish, if you forget lures, or if you wander out of the safe turtle stretch and waste casts. That estimate is mine, based on the vendor floors below and an unattended cast rate typical of a max-skill fisher. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/fishing-leveling-1-300-wow-classic))

**When to use it:**
- **Monday–Wednesday:** stock raw fish and cook only what beats the vendor floor.
- **Thursday–Sunday:** post raid food first; this is where Tanaris shines.

4. **AH price crashed?** Stop selling the cooked item, sell raw at vendor floor, and hold the stack until raid demand returns. That is the only sane response to a dead market.

## Failure modes: the 3 ways you still underperform

### 1) **You fish the wrong time window**
Nightfin is night-only in practice, and Sunscale is day-only in practice. If you ignore the clock, you cut your good fish rate hard and turn a great spot into trash. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

### 2) **You treat Bay of Storms like an AFK farm**
It isn’t. There are wandering mobs, and Wowhead explicitly warns that you need buffs and lures just to fish there comfortably. This is how people die or lose uptime while they’re trying to be clever. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

### 3) **You cook everything by habit instead of by margin**
Some fish are real money engines; some are only barely better than raw; some are raid-food plays only. If you auto-cook without checking the spread, you’ll waste spices, lose AH flexibility, and often make less than the raw floor would have given you. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/item%3D4603/raw-spotted-yellowtail?utm_source=openai))
