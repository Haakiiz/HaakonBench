This is not a fishing guide. This is an asset management manual. 

As a Level 60 Human Warrior with 300 Fishing and 300 Cooking, you are operating with two massive advantages: unlimited time, and the ability to process raw materials into raid-tier consumables. However, your class lacks stealth, meaning you are a sitting duck when truly AFK (defined here as clicking purely via sound cues while reading, working, or watching a movie, with zero situational awareness).

To optimize this, we must completely eliminate the variables of world PvP, mob respawns, and Horde griefing, while maximizing the Auction House Economy Cycle (AEC).

Here is the blueprint.

### I. THE COOKING FORCE MULTIPLIER

At 300 Cooking, your stove is a money printer. Selling raw fish is for bots and peasants. However, not all fish are created equal. You must know exactly what to cook, what to leave raw, and what to vendor.

**The "Holy Trinity" of Profitable Fish:**

*   **Winter Squid → Grilled Squid (+10 Agility)**
    *   *The Math:* Raw Winter Squid sells for ~15-20s. Cooked Grilled Squid sells for 60-90s per unit (12-18g per stack) on a mature server.
    *   *The Catch:* Winter Squid can **only** be caught between the Fall Equinox (September) and the Spring Equinox (March). If you are fishing in summer, you get Summer Bass (worthless).
*   **Raw Nightfin Snapper → Nightfin Soup (+8 MP5)**
    *   *The Math:* Raw Nightfin goes for ~20s. Cooked Nightfin Soup sells for 45-65s per unit (9-13g per stack). Healers burn through these on progression nights.
*   **Deviate Fish → Savory Deviate Delight (Cosmetic)**
    *   *The Math:* Raw Deviate Fish sells for 10s. SDD sells for 35-50s per unit (7-10g per stack). *Requirement:* You must buy the Recipe: Savory Deviate Delight. It is an upfront capital expense that pays for itself in two days.

**The "Do Not Cook" Exception:**
*   **Raw Stonescale Eel:** *Do not cook this.* There is no cooking recipe for it. It is an Alchemy reagent used for Stonescale Oil (Titans, Supreme Power). Sells raw for 50-80s *each* (10-16g per stack). 

**The Vendor Trash (Be Ruthless):**
*   Spotted Yellowtail, Glossy Mightfish, Redgill, Firefin Snapper (unless your server has a weirdly active Fire Protection Potion market, which usually crashes post-MC). Vendor them immediately. Do not waste AH deposit fees.

### II. THE AEC ROTATION (Auction House Economy Cycle)

Do not fish randomly. The WoW Classic economy operates on a strict weekly heartbeat dictated by raid lockouts and weekend casual play.

**Monday / Tuesday: The Consumable Push**
*   *Market State:* Progression guilds are preparing for Tuesday night resets. Demand for Nightfin Soup and Grilled Squid is at its absolute peak. 
*   *Your Action:* Empty your weekend inventory into the AH. Fish for **Nightfin Snapper** (Midnight to 12:00 PM server time) to catch the last-minute desperate buyers.

**Wednesday / Thursday: The Alchemy Restock**
*   *Market State:* Guilds have wiped, burned their flasks, and need to restock. 
*   *Your Action:* Farm **Stonescale Eels**. The market for raw fish dips slightly, but alchemy reagents remain stable. 

**Friday / Saturday / Sunday: The Casual Surge**
*   *Market State:* High server population, maximum AH saturation, prices crash on hardcore raid consumables because the supply from bots/casuals floods the market. 
*   *Your Action:* Farm **Deviate Fish**. The weekend brings out casual players, PvPers, and roleplayers who buy Savory Deviate Delights impulsively. Stockpile your Nightfin/Squid/Eels in your bank for Tuesday.
