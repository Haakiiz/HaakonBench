Even with 300 Fishing and 300 Cooking, players lose gold by making three common errors:

### 1. The "Sunscale Trap" (AFK Fishing at the Wrong Hour)
*   **The Mistake**: Leaving your character fishing in inland waters (Moonglade, Maraudon, Hinterlands) between **06:00 and 18:00**.

*   **The Fix**: Never AFK fish inland waters during the day. If you must fish between 06:00 and 18:00, move to **Feathermoon Stronghold Docks** for ocean catches (Squid, Eel, Yellowtail).

*   **The Mistake**: Fishing high-level end-game waters (Bay of Storms, Maraudon deeps, Feralas ocean) with only base 300 Fishing skill and no lure.

*   **The Reality**: Flooding the market crashes the price floor instantly. Competitors will undercut you by 1 copper, leaving your stacks unbought while deposit fees drain your gold.
