# Part II — The Auction House Economy Cycle

Fishing profit is realized when something sells, not when it enters your bags.

## Weekly schedule for a US-style Tuesday reset

### Monday morning: accumulate, do not dump

**Best activity:**

1. Scan raw fish and cooked food.
2. Buy severely underpriced Winter Squid, Nightfin or Greater Sagefish.
3. Fish whichever target has the best *expected net value per catch*.
4. Cook only enough for Tuesday.

Monday morning usually has weak immediate raid demand and leftover weekend stock. It is a production and acquisition window, not your main liquidation window.

### Tuesday afternoon and evening: primary sale window

Post:

- Grilled Squid in stacks of 5, 10 and 20.
- Nightfin Soup in stacks of 5 and 20.
- Stonescale Eel raw, primarily in stacks of 20.
- Oily Blackmouth raw in stacks of 10 and 20.

Start roughly **60–120 minutes before your realm’s major raid-start time**. Do not post your entire inventory at once.

### Wednesday evening: second-best sale window

Repost Tuesday leftovers, but reduce quantity. Wednesday buyers are often replenishing after consuming Tuesday’s stock.

### Thursday and Friday: fish, convert and stock

Good production days. List small quantities only unless your scan shows thin supply.

### Saturday evening: do not chase pools

Saturday has both demand and competition. Because this character cannot babysit the game:

- Use stationary open water.
- Let pool-chasers compete with one another.
- Post small batches at higher margins.
- Cancel and undercut only once, not every five minutes.

### Sunday evening: second liquidation wave

Sell remaining cooked raid food. Do not dump seasonal stock unless the price is excellent.

Use these hard rules:

### Grilled Squid crashes

### Nightfin Soup crashes

Sell raw Nightfin if the cooked premium is below **15% after the AH cut**. Soup moves faster, but raw fish appeal to both cooks and speculators.

### Stonescale Eel crashes

Pivot first to Nightfin, then to Oily Blackmouth. Eels and Blackmouth serve different consumable chains, so this diversifies you away from one saturated market. Stonescale Eel feeds Stonescale Oil and high-end alchemy demand; Blackmouth Oil is used in Free Action Potions and other recipes. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13423/stonescale-oil?utm_source=openai))

### Everything crashes

Do not keep producing because “fishing is free.”

- Bank seasonal squid.
- Vendor worthless cooked food/bycatch.
- Post only one or two stacks per product.
- Spend the session fishing Blackmouth in Westfall, whose use case differs from raid-stat food.
- If Blackmouth also fails your minimum price, stop fishing and liquidate inventory.

Unlimited time does not make zero-demand goods valuable.

# Part IV — Cooking as a force multiplier

## Pricing standard

Because you did not specify a realm, the figures below are **benchmark examples for a medium-population, non-hyperinflated economy**, not claimed live quotes.

The correct calculation is:

```text
Cooked net =
(cooked buyout × 0.95)
− vendor reagents
− expected deposit losses

Cooking uplift =
cooked net − raw buyout
```

Cook only when the uplift is at least:

- **15%** for fast sellers such as Nightfin Soup.
- **25%** for slower products.
- **40%** for obscure food with poor sale velocity.

# Part VI — The top three failure modes

## 1. Calculating production value instead of sale value

The biggest lie in fishing guides is:

> “I caught 30g worth of fish, therefore I made 30g.”

No. You acquired inventory theoretically listed at 30g.

If only half sells, you made approximately half that amount minus deposits and AH cuts. Darkclaw Lobster is the main trap: it can dominate your catch basket while contributing almost nothing to realized income.

**Fix:** Record sold value, not posted value, for seven days.

---

## 2. Fishing the correct zone at the wrong clock

Players routinely fish Nightfin at 2:00 p.m. local time while the server clock is in the noon-to-6:00 p.m. exclusion window. They also farm “Winter” Squid after the spring equinox or fish squid at night when midday would be better. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13759/raw-nightfin-snapper?utm_source=openai))

**Fix:** Put server time next to the minimap and make the decision from that clock.

---

## 3. Treating “safe” as a zone property

Azshara is not safe. A particular empty rock in Azshara may be environmentally safe.

Steamwheedle Port is not PvP-safe. Goblin guards reacting after you are attacked do not satisfy a no-death requirement.

Moonglade is peaceful, not a sanctuary.

Westfall is safe only if your PvP flag is down and you park away from murloc/Defias geometry.

**Fix:** Before an extended session:

1. Stand still for five minutes.
2. Observe every patrol.
3. Confirm your PvP flag is gone.
4. Check that no spawn point is beneath or behind you.
5. Only then begin fishing.
