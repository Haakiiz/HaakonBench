# The no-death, no-babysit fishing guide for a level 60 Alliance Warrior

**Hard rule:** if you truly cannot die, you are not a “fisherman”; you are a **stationary shoreline investor**. You do **not** chase pools, you do **not** fish Bay of Storms, you do **not** stand near hostile patrols, and you do **not** rely on being level 60 in plate. Plate does not save an unattended Warrior.

Also: “AFK-safe” here means **your character can stand there unattended without mobs killing you**. It does **not** mean automated fishing. You still have to play the game when you fish.

Today is **July 9, 2026**, so for Classic-era seasonal fishing you are in **Summer Bass season**, not Winter Squid season. Winter Squid returns around the fall equinox window, roughly **September 22/23**, and leaves around **March 19/20**; Summer Bass is the opposite season. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow))

## 0. The ruthless safety filter

### If you are on a PvE / Normal / Hardcore PvE realm
Use the full guide. Do **not** flag for PvP. Do not help flagged players. Do not AoE near flagged enemies. Your real risk is hostile mobs and bad positioning.

### If you are on a PvP realm and “cannot die” is literal
Your profitable high-end route collapses. Contested-zone fishing is not no-death-safe. A bored rogue can kill you while you are in the shower. Your safe route becomes **Menethil Harbor / Darkshore / Stormwind canals**, and your gold/hour drops hard.

That is not a caveat; it is the truth. Any guide recommending Azshara, Tanaris, Feralas, or Moonglade as “AFK-safe” on a PvP realm is lying to you.

# 1. The route engine: what to fish, when, and why

You make money by syncing three cycles:

1. **Fish biology** — Nightfin, Sunscale, Stonescale, Squid/Bass timing.
2. **Auction House Economy Cycle — AEC** — raid reset and weekend oversupply.
3. **Your safety requirement** — stationary, mob-free, no patrol gambling.

US weekly reset is Tuesday, so Tuesday/Wednesday raid-prep demand matters most on US realms. ([us.forums.blizzard.com](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/server-weekly-reset-time/146785?utm_source=openai))

## Weekly AEC rotation

### Monday morning
**Do not dump fish.**  
Monday morning is usually where weekend fishers have already undercut each other. Your job:

- Fish **Nightfin** if it is midnight–6.
- Fish **Steamwheedle coastal** otherwise.
- Buy underpriced raw Nightfin, Stonescale, Winter Squid stockpiles, and cheap cooked consumables.
- Cook only if the cooked premium exists.

### Monday evening
Start listing **small stacks**:

- Nightfin Soup: stacks of **5 and 10**.
- Stonescale Eel: stacks of **10 and 20**.
- Grilled Squid, if you have old stock: stacks of **5/10/20**.

Do not flood. You are baiting Tuesday buyers.

### Tuesday reset day
This is your highest-confidence selling window.

- List Nightfin Soup before raid prime time.
- List Stonescale Eel before alchemists and flask/prep buyers arrive.
- If you have Grilled Squid stock from winter, Tuesday evening is a premium sell window.

### Wednesday / Thursday
Continue selling, but undercut gently. If the market is crowded, fish instead of fighting.

### Friday
Mostly gather. Casual demand exists, but serious raid-prep demand is weaker.

### Saturday daytime
**Do not sell into the weekend farmer wall** unless prices are weirdly high. Fish, cook, bank.

### Saturday evening
If raid guilds on your server commonly raid Saturday, list **only your highest-margin cooked goods**. Otherwise keep banking.

### Sunday evening
Good secondary demand. List Nightfin Soup and Stonescale in small batches. Do not list your entire inventory.

# 2. The decision tree

## Step 1 — Are you on a PvP realm?

### Yes, and death is unacceptable
Go to **Menethil Harbor**. That is your main route. If Menethil prices crash, fish **Stormwind canals** for ultra-safe trash/cooking fish or Darkshore/Menethil open water for Oily Blackmouth/Firefin.

### No, or PvE/Hardcore PvE unflagged
Use the real money route below.

---

## Step 2 — What time is it?

### 00:00–06:00
1. If **Nightfin Soup sells for at least raw Nightfin + 1g per stack**, fish **Moonglade**.
2. If Nightfin/Soup is crashed, fish **Steamwheedle Port** for Stonescale.
3. If both are crashed, fish **Menethil** and bank alchemy fish until Tuesday.

### 06:00–12:00
1. Fish **Steamwheedle Port**.
2. If Steamwheedle is visually annoying/camped, go **Feathermoon dock**.
3. If both markets are weak, Menethil.

### 12:00–18:00
1. Fish **Moonglade Sunscale** only if Poached Sunscale Salmon is selling.
2. Otherwise fish **Steamwheedle** for coastal bycatch.
3. On PvP/no-death mode, Menethil only.

### 18:00–24:00
1. Start Moonglade Nightfin.
2. Switch to Steamwheedle only if Stonescale is above your server’s normal price.

| Market crash | Pivot |

# 3. Recommended AFK-safe fishing spots

Gold/hour assumes roughly **120–140 successful catches/hour**, no pool chasing, no botting, a mature mid-pop Classic economy, 5% AH cut, and normal relisting friction. These are honest ranges, not fake spreadsheet precision.

---

## A. Moonglade — Lake Elune’ara / Nighthaven shoreline

**Use for:** Nightfin Snapper, Sunscale Salmon, inland bycatch.  
**Best time:** **00:00–06:00** for Nightfin; **12:00–18:00** for Sunscale.  
Nightfin is an inland fish and can be fished in Moonglade; Nightfin is strongest midnight–6 and absent noon–6, while Sunscale runs the opposite day cycle. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13759/raw-nightfin-snapper?utm_source=openai))

### Gold/hour

| Time | Expected value |
|---|---:|
| 00:00–06:00 Nightfin | **12–26g/hour** |
| 18:00–24:00 Nightfin ramp | **8–18g/hour** |
| 12:00–18:00 Sunscale | **4–12g/hour** |

### Why it is AFK-safe
- Fish from the developed Nighthaven/Lake Elune’ara shoreline, not random wilderness edges.
- No meaningful hostile patrol pressure if you choose the town-facing shore.
- You are not moving between pools.
- Nearest flight access is Nighthaven if you have it.
- On PvE/Hardcore PvE, stay unflagged and enemy players cannot simply murder you.
- On PvP realms, this is **not** no-death-safe because Moonglade is not an Alliance home-zone sanctuary for your purposes.

### What collapses the estimate
- Fishing outside the Nightfin window.
- Selling raw Nightfin when Soup has a premium.
- Listing all Soup on Saturday afternoon.
- Too many casters/healers already supplied after raid night.

### Cooking play
**Raw Nightfin Snapper → Nightfin Soup** is one of the best fish conversions in Classic. Nightfin Soup restores health and gives **8 mana per 5 seconds for 10 minutes**, making it desirable for healers and mana users. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13931/nightfin-soup?utm_source=openai))

Typical valuation:

| Item | Typical stack value | Action |
|---|---:|---|
| Raw Nightfin Snapper | **4–8g/stack** | Sell raw only if Soup premium is gone. |
| Nightfin Soup | **6–12g/stack** | Default: cook and sell. |
| Net cooking lift | **+2–4g/stack** | Worth doing. |

**Math:** if you catch ~30 Nightfin/hour and Soup adds ~2–4g/stack, Cooking adds roughly **3–6g/hour** by itself.

### What collapses the estimate
- Fishing outside eel-favored night hours.
- Treating Summer Bass as if it were Winter Squid.

## C. Feathermoon Stronghold dock, Feralas

**Use for:** coastal seasonal fish, Glossy Mightfish, Yellowtail-style bycatch.  
**Best use:** Winter Squid season, or when Steamwheedle is annoying.

### Gold/hour

| Season | Expected value |
|---|---:|
| July / Summer Bass season | **4–11g/hour** |
| Winter Squid season | **8–25g/hour** |

### Why it is AFK-safe
- You fish from the Alliance stronghold/dock area.
- No normal hostile patrol should path onto your fishing position if you stay inside the developed dock/town footprint.
- Flight point and vendors are in the same Alliance hub.
- It is much safer than wandering Feralas shorelines.
- On PvP realms, still not no-death-safe: Feralas is contested-world PvP territory.

### What collapses the estimate
- Fishing it in July expecting Squid.
- Cooking Summer Bass into Hot Smoked Bass when nobody wants it.
- Trying to compete with Steamwheedle’s broader bycatch when Steamwheedle is safe and available.

### Cooking play
Same as Steamwheedle: **Winter Squid is the prize**, Summer Bass is filler. Cook Squid; test Bass.

## D. Menethil Harbor, Wetlands — the contrarian insurance spot

Most gold guides dismiss Menethil because it is not glamorous. For this character, Menethil is underrated because it is **actually AFK-safe**, Alliance-convenient, and produces alchemy fish that sell steadily when raid consumable fish are overfarmed.

**Use for:** Oily Blackmouth, Firefin Snapper, lower-tier coastal bycatch. Oily Blackmouth is used for Blackmouth Oil and is specifically associated with Menethil docks; Firefin pools are also reported around Menethil/Wetlands. ([warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Oily_Blackmouth?utm_source=openai))

### Gold/hour

| Mode | Expected value |
|---|---:|
| Stationary open-water Menethil | **2–6g/hour** |
| Strong PvP/SoD-style demand for oils | **5–10g/hour** |
| Dead alchemy market | **1–3g/hour** |

### Why it is AFK-safe
- Alliance harbor, guards, inn, vendors, boats, and flight point nearby.
- No hostile mob patrol should reach you on the main docks.
- On PvP realms, this is one of the few realistic no-death fishing options because you are in Alliance-controlled territory.
- If you need to step away mid-session, Menethil is vastly safer than Tanaris, Feralas, Azshara, or STV.

### What collapses the estimate
- Oily Blackmouth below 1g/stack.
- Firefin below 1g/stack.
- Trying to sell massive stacks on the weekend.
- Ignoring alchemists: these are reagent fish, not cooked-food fish.

### Cooking play
None.  
**Oily Blackmouth and Firefin Snapper have no profitable Cooking conversion for you.** Sell raw to alchemists. If the AH is below vendor-level nonsense, bank until Tuesday/Wednesday.

# 6. What to do when a zone is “camped”

Because you are stationary open-water fishing, another fisher usually does **not** ruin your catch. “Camped” only matters if:

- their bobbers visually interfere with yours,
- they drag mobs,
- they are PvP griefing,
- or the AH is camped with undercuts.

### If Moonglade is crowded
Move 20–40 yards along the safe town-facing shore. Do **not** go exploring into hostile pathing just to get solitude.

### If Steamwheedle is crowded
Move slightly north along the safe coastline. Do **not** go south toward Wastewander mobs.

### If Feathermoon is crowded
Use Steamwheedle unless it is Winter Squid season and Steamwheedle is also packed.

### If Menethil is crowded
Stay. Open water does not deplete. Menethil is your safety bunker.

# 7. One contrarian recommendation

## Menethil Harbor is better than most guides admit

Most guides chase top theoretical gold/hour: Azshara, Bay of Storms, STV pools, Tanaris pool loops. Those are better **if you are actively playing**.

For this specific character — level 60 Warrior, cannot die, cannot babysit — **Menethil Harbor is the best crash-resistant fallback in the game**.

Why?

- It is Alliance-controlled.
- It has docks with no hostile mob pathing.
- Oily Blackmouth and Firefin have alchemy demand rather than food demand.
- It performs best exactly when raid-food markets are overfarmed.
- It is PvP-realm compatible in a way Tanaris/Feralas/Moonglade are not.
- It lets you step away without wondering if a turtle, naga, giant, rogue, or patrol is about to end your character.

No, it will not beat perfect Nightfin or Stonescale hours. But it beats being dead. For this guide, that matters more.

# 8. Failure modes: why players following this guide still underperform

## 1. They fish the right fish at the wrong time
Nightfin at 2 PM is a self-own. Winter Squid in July is impossible. Summer Bass is not Grilled Squid. The biological clock matters more than the zone name.

## 2. They cook everything
Cooking is not automatically profit.  
**Nightfin Soup and Grilled Squid are businesses.**  
Spotted Yellowtail, Baked Salmon, Lobster Stew, Rockscale Cod, and Redgill are usually not.

## 3. They sell into supply instead of demand
Saturday afternoon is when bored players create cheap fish. Tuesday evening is when raiders buy consumables. If you fish Saturday and dump Saturday, you are working for Tuesday sellers.
