# The No-Death, Low-Attention Fishing Plan  
## Level 60 Human Warrior • Alliance • 300 Fishing / 300 Cooking • Classic-era rules

First, the hard truth:

> **You cannot literally fish while AFK.** Every catch requires a new cast and a bobber click; automating that is botting.  
> This guide uses **AFK-safe** correctly: places where you can step away from the keyboard, shower, answer the door, or stare at a second monitor **without an NPC pathing over and killing you**.

There is one absolute distinction:

- **Normal/PvE realm:** this guide works. Keep PvP disabled and enemy players cannot attack you unless you flag yourself.
- **PvP realm:** there is **no profitable genuinely shower-safe outdoor fishing location**. Moonglade and Feralas are contested; on a PvP realm, entering contested territory flags you and makes you attackable. Your only true unattended-safe option is a friendly capital—and its fish are not serious income. ([us.media.blizzard.com](https://us.media.blizzard.com/manuals/wow/wow-classic-manual-enUS.pdf?utm_source=openai))

So the best possible answer is not “fish Azshara.” It is a **two-spot, time-based production route** on a normal realm, with a capital fallback and strict market rules.

# The Only Two Profitable AFK-Safe Spots I Recommend

## 1. Lake Elune’ara, Moonglade — The Default Money Spot

### Stand here
Fish from the **Nighthaven-side bank of Lake Elune’ara**, staying near the settlement rather than wandering to remote shoreline sections.

### What you catch
- **00:00–06:00 server time:** Raw Nightfin Snapper is the target.
- **12:00–18:00:** Raw Sunscale Salmon replaces much of the Nightfin value.
- Side catch: Raw Redgill, Raw Mithril Head Trout, Oily Blackmouth, occasional trophy fish.

Player-recorded samples at Lake Elune’ara show roughly 29–35% Nightfin during the prime night window, with Redgill making up much of the remainder. That is exactly why this spot works: it has a dependable high-value target, not a miracle catch table. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13759/raw-nightfin-snapper?utm_source=openai))

### Why it is AFK-safe
**PvE/normal realm only:**
- The productive bank is immediately beside Nighthaven, not a random wilderness pool.
- There are no normal hostile shoreline patrols expected to aggro a level 60 character at that bank.
- The nearest Alliance flight point is in **Nighthaven**, a few seconds away.
- You can cook, vendor junk, and leave via flight without crossing hostile mob territory.
- An enemy player cannot kill an unflagged player on a normal realm. ([us.media.blizzard.com](https://us.media.blizzard.com/manuals/wow/wow-classic-manual-enUS.pdf?utm_source=openai))

**PvP-realm warning:** Moonglade is explicitly contested territory. Do **not** treat its druid theme or nearby guards as a force field. A rogue can kill you while you shower. ([warcraft.wiki.gg](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Moonglade?utm_source=openai))

### Honest gold/hour
**18–55g/hour realized value**, with these assumptions:

- 120–160 successful casts/hour while actively clicking.
- 25–35% Nightfin in the 00:00–06:00 window.
- Nightfin Soup sells at a meaningful premium over raw Nightfin.
- You sell in stacks of 10 or 20 on the faction AH.
- The market is not saturated by other fishers.

**What collapses it to 6–15g/hour:**
1. You fish at noon expecting Nightfin.
2. Nightfin Soup is undercut to raw-fish price.
3. You dump every ancillary fish on the AH instead of vendoring the trash and listing only saleable stacks.
4. You price in singles and repeatedly lose deposits.

### The play
- **Night window:** stay here until you have 40–80 Raw Nightfin Snapper.
- Cook only if the soup premium clears the conversion test below.
- Vendor Raw Mithril Head Trout and low-value food unless your AH proves otherwise.
- Sell Oily Blackmouth raw in stacks of 20 if it has alchemy demand; otherwise vendor it.

This is the spot you use when you want income without risk, drama, or a second monitor devoted to watching your health bar.

## 2. Feathermoon Stronghold Dock, Feralas — The Eel Pivot

### Stand here
Fish from the **protected dock/settlement-side water at Feathermoon Stronghold**. Do not ride to an exposed beach because a guide told you the coast is “better.”

### What you catch
Primary target:
- **Stonescale Eel**, particularly at night.

Possible profitable side outcomes:
- Winter Squid in season.
- Raw Summer Bass in the opposite season.
- Raw Spotted Yellowtail.
- Raw Glossy Mightfish.
- Firefin Snapper, Oily Blackmouth, and general coastal filler.

Stonescale Eel is a reagent fish: it becomes Stonescale Oil, used in major alchemy outputs including Flask of the Titans, Greater Stoneshield Potion, and Potion of Petrification. That makes its price depend more on raiders and alchemists than on food-buff buyers. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item%3D13422/stonescale-eel?utm_source=openai))

### Why it is AFK-safe
**PvE/normal realm only:**
- You fish from an Alliance settlement dock rather than the Feralas coastline.
- There is no reason for hostile Feralas mobs to patrol onto the Stronghold dock.
- Your nearest Alliance flight point, inn, guards, vendors, and cooking access are all inside the Stronghold.
- You can leave the character standing at the dock without a land mob’s respawn geometry becoming a surprise death.

**PvP-realm warning:** Feralas is contested. Stronghold guards are not a guarantee against a prepared enemy player, especially a stealthed opener. This is a “safe from NPCs” spot, **not** a PvP-safe spot.

### Honest gold/hour
**14–45g/hour realized value**, assuming:
- Night fishing.
- 7–16 Stonescale Eels/hour from open-water mixed catches.
- Eels sell into a live alchemy market rather than a flooded weekend market.
- You retain seasonal/high-value fish and vendor low-value catches.

**What collapses it below 10g/hour:**
- You fish it at low-demand daytime hours.
- Eel prices have been crushed by concentrated pool farmers.
- You cook every fish indiscriminately.
- You fish outside the Stronghold trying to chase a marginally better table, then lose an hour—or your character—to a death.

### When Feathermoon beats Moonglade
Go here only when **one** of these is true:

1. **Stonescale Eel net price is at least 1.5× Nightfin Soup net price per expected catch.**
2. Nightfin Soup has been undercut to near-raw value.
3. It is Winter Squid season and cooked squid is being banked for later resale.
4. You are online during 00:00–06:00 server time and want a higher-variance alchemy play.

# The Schedule: Fish the Economy, Not Just the Water

## Monday morning: manufacture, do not liquidate
Fish **Nightfin at Moonglade from 00:00–06:00 server time**.

Monday is for building inventory because the prior weekend’s casual sellers often leave cheap raw fish on the AH. Buy underpriced raw Nightfin, cook it if profitable, and hold your finished food for the next buyer window.

**Rule:** do not post your whole stock Monday morning unless the market is already thin.

---

## Tuesday reset / raid-prep evening: sell consumables
List:
- Nightfin Soup
- Grilled Squid
- Stonescale Eel
- Stonescale Oil only if you have an alchemist; otherwise sell the eel

Use **stacks of 10** for raid consumables. Ten is the practical compromise: affordable enough for an individual player, large enough that you are not creating deposit and mailbox clutter.

Faction AH sales lose 5% of the sale price; neutral AH sales lose 15%. Deposits are forfeited if the item expires, so do not repeatedly repost low-margin cooked food. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/classic-auction-house-economy?utm_source=openai))

---

## Friday night: build the weekend raid-food wall
- Fish **Moonglade Nightfin** if soup stock is low.
- Fish **Feathermoon Eels** if alchemy demand is stronger.
- In Winter Squid season, take squid seriously.

Winter Squid is season-limited—roughly late September through late March—and is the input for Grilled Squid, a +10 Agility food. The correct play is often to fish and cook it during season, then sell it during the months when nobody can catch it. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow?utm_source=openai))

---

## Saturday evening: sell, don’t farm
Saturday evening is when impatient buyers are online.

Do this:
- Post only 30–50% of your inventory.
- Keep the remainder in the bank.
- Let undercutters burn through their stock first.
- Repost once; do not enter a cancel-repost war over fish.

If there are five pages of Nightfin Soup below your floor, **do not compete.** Go fish Stonescale Eel, hold raw Nightfin, or log off. A fisherman who sells into a collapse is not farming gold; he is financing other players’ cheap consumables.

# The Cooking Ledger: What to Cook, What to Sell Raw, What to Vendor

Use this formula before cooking:

\[
\textbf{Cook if }(0.95 \times \text{cooked AH price}) - \text{vendor reagents} > (0.95 \times \text{raw AH price})
\]

The 0.95 accounts for the faction-AH cut. Do not use neutral AH for normal fish commerce. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/classic-auction-house-economy?utm_source=openai))

# The Contrarian Recommendation: Moonglade Is Better Than Azshara for This Character

Most fishing guides eventually point you to **Azshara’s Bay of Storms** for giant mixed catches, lobster, squid, eel, and mightfish.

For this character’s actual constraints, that advice is bad.

Bay of Storms has:
- wandering high-level mobs,
- exposed rocks and islands,
- a 330 minimum skill requirement just to fish there,
- up to 425 effective Fishing needed to avoid get-aways,
- and no PvP safety whatsoever on a PvP realm. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow?utm_source=openai))

Yes, its ceiling is higher. Its **risk-adjusted, attention-adjusted, no-death value** is worse.

**Moonglade Nightfin is the contrarian winner** because it converts your constraint—low attention—into an advantage:
- predictable time window,
- settlement-adjacent position,
- easy exit,
- high-demand cooked output,
- no lure dependency,
- no pressure to chase pools,
- no temptation to ride into dangerous coastline for a theoretical 10% improvement.

The best fishing spot is not where the spreadsheet’s top catch exists. It is where you can repeatedly fish for two hours without ever needing to look at your Warrior’s health bar.

# When a Spot Is “Camped”

## If another fisherman is present
At open water, another fisherman usually does **not** deplete your personal catch table. Do not leave merely because someone else is there.

Leave only if:
- they are physically obstructing your bobber,
- they are trying to bait a PvP flag,
- they are a hostile player on a PvP realm,
- they are creating enough attention burden that the spot no longer meets the purpose of the guide.

## Pivot order
1. **Moonglade Nightfin** if it is 00:00–06:00.
2. **Feathermoon Eels** if Nightfin pricing is weak.
3. **Moonglade Sunscale** in the daytime only if cooked Salmon clears the margin test.
4. **Stormwind canal or log out** if you genuinely must leave the keyboard on a PvP realm.

Do **not** pivot to Azshara, Eastern Plaguelands, Tanaris beach, or a remote Feralas shore while you are trying to preserve a no-death character. Those are attended-farming locations, not AFK-safe locations.

# The Three Ways You Will Still Underperform

## 1. Fishing on your clock instead of server time
Nightfin at noon is not “bad luck.” It is the wrong table. Sunscale at 02:00 is the same mistake. Set your play around **server time**, not your local time. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/fishing-leveling-1-300-wow-classic?utm_source=openai))

## 2. Cooking everything because you have 300 Cooking
Cooking is not automatically value creation.  
If a cooked item lacks a desired buff—or the cooked premium does not exceed spices, AH cut, and raw opportunity cost—you are converting a saleable reagent into slow-moving vendor food.

## 3. Mistaking “guarded” for “PvP safe”
Feathermoon and Nighthaven are excellent **normal-realm** low-attention sites. They are not immunity bubbles on a PvP realm. If death is unacceptable, your rule is simple:

> **On a PvP realm, never leave a level-60 character unattended outside a friendly capital.**

# Final Operating Rules

1. **Nightfin is your baseline business.**
2. **Stonescale is your volatility hedge.**
3. **Winter Squid is your seasonal speculation.**
4. **Cook only when the cooked margin beats raw sale after the 5% cut and reagents.**
5. **Sell food on raid-prep windows; fish during quiet windows.**
6. **Never chase Bay of Storms while operating under a no-death / low-attention constraint.**
7. **If you need to be truly AFK, log out.** No fish is worth discovering that one wandering mob had a 60-yard patrol.
