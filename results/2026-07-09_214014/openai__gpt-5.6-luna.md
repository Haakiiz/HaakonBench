# The Zero-Death Fishing Route for a Level 60 Alliance Warrior

**Assumption:** WoW Classic Era, not Season of Discovery, Hardcore, or Cataclysm Classic. Prices below are **planning ranges for a medium/high-population realm**, not a promise about your server. Classic fish prices are extraordinarily realm-specific.

The central rule is simple:

> **If you are genuinely going AFK for hours, only capital-city fishing is completely safe.**  
> Outdoor fishing can be NPC-safe, but on a PvP realm no beach, dock, or “quiet” cave is safe from an enemy player walking up and killing or repeatedly interrupting you.

That distinction is more important than finding a theoretically high-value pool.

Use this every time you log in.

# 2. The Weekly Rotation

The route is not “go to the best zone.” It is:

- exploit time-sensitive fish,
- avoid overfilling the market,
- sell food when raiders and PvPers are buying,
- switch to alchemy reagents when food is saturated.

## Monday morning: Stonescale Eel and alchemy reagents

### Primary: Tanaris — Steamwheedle Port

Fish from the docks and shoreline around Steamwheedle Port.

**Target:**

- Stonescale Eel
- Wreckage pools
- Oily Blackmouth
- Firefin Snapper

Stonescale Eel is used for Stonescale Oil, which feeds important alchemy consumables. Oily Blackmouth and Firefin Snapper likewise feed oils used in potions and other crafts. ([icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-classic/classic-fishing-profession-guide?utm_source=openai))

### Why Monday?

Raiders and guild crafters replenish consumables after the weekend. Monday is usually better for selling alchemy inputs than Friday night, when many sellers dump their weekend stock.

### Safety

- **NPC safety:** Steamwheedle Port is much safer than the open Tanaris coastline. The dock itself has no normal hostile mob patrol through the fishing positions.
- **Terrain:** Stand on the dock, not on the beach. This prevents wandering desert mobs from reaching you.
- **Flight point:** Gadgetzan is the nearest practical flight point, though it is neutral rather than Alliance-controlled.
- **PvP warning:** On a PvP server, this is not AFK-safe. Horde players can reach the port, and a player can kill or interrupt you without the goblin guards reliably making the situation safe.

### Expected yield

**NPC-safe/PvE estimate:** **8–18 gold per hour gross**, assuming:

- 250–350 casts per hour,
- a reasonable number of pools,
- moderate Stonescale demand,
- some wreckage or secondary fish,
- no major AH saturation.

**PvP or contested server:** treat this as **0–12 gold per hour**, because a single interruption destroys the session.

## Tuesday–Wednesday: Winter Squid and high-value cooked food

### Primary: Azshara coastline

Fish the coastal schools around Azshara, especially the shoreline near the southern and central coast rather than spending all your time in the heavily traveled northern areas.

**Target:**

- Winter Squid
- Firefin Snapper
- Oily Blackmouth
- Stonescale Eel at appropriate times

Winter Squid is the main reason to fish Azshara in the relevant season. It becomes Grilled Squid, a 10 Agility food, and is one of the strongest fish-to-food conversions available to a max-level cook. The recipe is learned at Cooking 240. ([wowhead.com](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/best-fishing-pools-farming-locations-classic-wow?utm_source=openai))

### Why Tuesday and Wednesday?

This is when many guilds are preparing for midweek raid nights. Grilled Squid buyers are less price-sensitive than ordinary leveling players.

### Safety

- **NPC safety:** The coast is usually safe if you fish from a cleared rock shelf or shallow shoreline away from naga camps.
- **Do not fish inside or immediately beside hostile naga camps.**
- **Patrols:** Avoid roads and quest areas. The main danger is not the water; it is wandering naga, satyrs, or summoned enemies near the shore.
- **Flight point:** Azshara has no convenient Alliance flight point near the prime coastline. The usual access is from **Talrendis Point in Azshara**, if available to you, or via adjacent Alliance travel routes. This makes it less attractive for repeated trips.
- **PvP:** Not AFK-safe. Azshara is a popular place for enemy players to find isolated targets.

### Expected yield

**PvE / watched character:** **10–24 gold per hour gross** during strong Winter Squid demand.

**Food-saturated realm:** **5–14 gold per hour.**

The estimate collapses when:

- Winter Squid is already below your raw-fish value after spices,
- several farmers list hundreds of cooked stacks,
- it is the wrong in-game season for Winter Squid,
- you are fishing empty shoreline instead of schools.

## Thursday: Nightfin Snapper and Poached Sunscale Salmon

### Primary: Feralas — Verdantis River

Fish from the river sections near Feathermoon rather than the exposed coastline.

The Verdantis River can produce Nightfin Snapper, Sunscale Salmon, Redgill, and secondary fish. Older fishing references specifically identify Feralas river fishing as a source of these fish, with the exact catch table changing by time of day. ([vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com](https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Fishing?utm_source=openai))

### Time of day

For this rotation, fish:

- **Night:** Nightfin Snapper
- **Day:** Sunscale Salmon and Redgill

Nightfin Soup provides mana regeneration, while Poached Sunscale Salmon provides health regeneration. Both recipes are available at Cooking 250. ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/classic/fishing-and-cooking-leveling-guide-classic-wow?utm_source=openai))

### Why Thursday?

Thursday is a good preparation day for raid groups and dungeon players. Nightfin tends to sell better when mana users are stocking consumables; Sunscale is more niche and should not be cooked automatically.

### Safety

- **NPC safety:** Choose a riverbank with no hostile mobs within casting range and no nearby quest camp.
- **Patrols:** Feralas has roaming beasts and hostile mobs. Do not fish beside roads or the Ruins of Dire Maul approach.
- **Nearest Alliance flight point:** **Feathermoon Stronghold** is the practical Alliance base.
- **Terrain:** Use the bank, not a tiny island or ledge where the character can slide into water and become stuck.
- **PvP:** Feralas is contested. Outdoor AFK fishing is not safe on PvP servers.

### Expected yield

**Nightfin-focused:** **7–18 gold per hour gross.**

**Sunscale-focused:** **4–11 gold per hour gross.**

Nightfin is usually the better product. Poached Sunscale Salmon is often a trap: it looks like an endgame food, but it does not always command enough of a premium to justify cooking.

## Friday: Cooked Glossy Mightfish and Mightfish Steak

### Primary: Azshara or Feralas coastal waters

Target:

- Raw Glossy Mightfish
- Large Raw Mightfish
- Stonescale Eel as secondary income

Cooked Glossy Mightfish grants stamina, while Mightfish Steak provides a stronger stamina food. These can be useful for tanks and players preparing for difficult content.

### Why Friday?

Friday evening is when:

- raiders buy consumables,
- PvPers stock food,
- casual players have time to play,
- AH turnover improves.

It is also when supply increases sharply. Do not dump your entire inventory at once. List in small batches.

### Expected yield

**7–16 gold per hour gross** if stamina food is moving.

If the AH is flooded, sell the fish raw or hold it for Monday rather than converting everything.

## Saturday evening: Oily Blackmouth and Firefin Snapper

### Primary: Western Plaguelands inland pools or coastal routes

Oily Blackmouth can appear in multiple lower-level coastal and inland locations, including Western Plaguelands and Un’Goro-related waters; Firefin is associated with oil slicks and similar fishing areas. ([icy-veins.com](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-classic/classic-fishing-profession-guide?utm_source=openai))

### Why Saturday evening?

Saturday is often the worst time to sell generic cooked food because farmers and casual players post heavily. Alchemy reagents can outperform food because crafters buy them in batches.

Use the AH before committing:

- If Oily Blackmouth is selling for more than your food-equivalent margin, fish it.
- If Oily Blackmouth is below the value of vendor trash plus cooked food, move to a different route.
- If both are saturated, fish in the capital city and preserve your time.

### Safety

Western Plaguelands is **not** truly AFK-safe:

- hostile undead patrols exist,
- roads have more traffic,
- enemy players may pass through,
- some inland pools are near hostile NPCs.

Use it only while watching the screen. On a PvE realm, stand far enough from roads and camps that no mob can path to you.

### Expected yield

**5–15 gold per hour gross**, highly dependent on alchemy demand.

The number collapses if:

- Free Action Potion production is low,
- Fire Protection Potion demand is low,
- alchemists are selling finished potions cheaply,
- the AH has several pages of fish listed below your target price.

## The recipes you should actively cook

### 1. Grilled Squid

**Example:**

- 20 Winter Squid: 5g
- 20 Soothing Spices: 0.3g
- Finished stack: 13g
- AH cut and deposit loss: approximately 0.8g

**Net:** approximately **6.9g profit per stack**

This is your strongest reliable fish-to-food conversion when the market supports it.

### 2. Nightfin Soup

**Example:**

- Raw Nightfin: 3g
- Water: negligible
- Finished stack: 8g
- AH costs: 0.5g

**Net:** approximately **4.5g per stack**

Nightfin Soup is particularly good because it has a clear raid-use case and cheap ingredients.

### 3. Sagefish Delight

- Raw Greater Sagefish: 3g
- Hot Spices: 0.3g
- Finished stack: 9g
- AH costs: 0.5g

**Net:** approximately **5.2g per stack**

If the finished food sells for less than raw fish plus spices, sell the fish raw.

# 4. What to Do With Fish That Have No Profitable Conversion

Do not cook just because your Cooking skill is 300.

## Vendor or sell raw when:

- the cooked item has no stat advantage,
- the cooked item is consumed only by leveling characters,
- the AH has more than 3–5 pages listed,
- cooked value is less than raw value plus ingredient cost,
- the fish is needed by Alchemy and raw buyers are paying a premium.

### Spotted Yellowtail

Spotted Yellowtail is a classic example of a fish that looks like it belongs in a cooking guide but often does not deserve your time. If the raw stack sells for 1.5g and the cooked stack sells for 1.7g, cooking it is not profitable after labor, deposits, and listing risk.

**Sell raw unless cooked food is at least 25–30% above raw value.**

### Poached Sunscale Salmon

Useful food does not automatically mean profitable food. If Nightfin Soup is moving at 8g per stack and Poached Sunscale is moving at 3g, fish Nightfin at night and sell Sunscale raw or hold it.

### Hot Smoked Bass

Usually a niche stamina/spirit product. Only cook it if the finished stack is clearly above raw value.

# 5. AH Rules That Actually Matter

## Use a three-price threshold

Before leaving town, record:

1. **Raw fish price**
2. **Cooked price**
3. **Vendor fallback**

Then apply this rule:

### Cook only if:

\[
\text{Cooked price} \geq \text{Raw price} + \text{ingredient cost} + 20\%
\]

That extra 20% pays for:

- relisting,
- deposits,
- unsold inventory,
- your time,
- market movement while you are fishing.

## Never post your entire haul

Post:

- 2–4 stacks of cooked food,
- 2–4 stacks of raw reagent fish,
- keep the remainder in your bank.

If your listing is undercut, do not blindly match it. Wait for the cheap stock to sell unless you are dealing with a crashing market.

## Market crash pivot

### If Grilled Squid crashes

Move to:

1. Nightfin Snapper
2. Stonescale Eel
3. Oily Blackmouth
4. City fishing if all three markets are saturated

### If Stonescale crashes

Fish Winter Squid or Nightfin. Stonescale is valuable because of downstream alchemy demand, but that demand is not constant.

### If all fish prices crash

Do not stubbornly fish the “best zone.” Your best route becomes:

- capital-city AFK fishing,
- cooking only for personal use,
- selling fish raw after the market recovers,
- gathering a different profession’s materials.

# 6. The Underrated Recommendation: Ironforge Canals

Most guides dismiss capital fishing because the fish are worthless.

For this character, that is too simplistic.

## Why Ironforge canals are underrated

You specified:

- unlimited time,
- cannot die,
- cannot babysit the game.

That makes **uptime** more valuable than theoretical yield.

Outdoor route:

- 15 gold/hour while watched,
- 0 gold/hour after being killed or disconnected,
- repeated travel and corpse recovery,
- possible PvP interruption.

Ironforge route:

- perhaps 1–3 gold/hour in vendorable fish,
- no travel,
- no deaths,
- no mobs,
- no enemy players,
- zero attention required.

If you are going to be away for six hours, Ironforge can outperform an outdoor route that requires monitoring. It also generates cooking materials, fishing skill practice, and vendor income without risking the character.

This is not the highest-gold fishing location. It is the best location under a **strict AFK constraint**.

# 8. The Three Failure Modes

## 1. Confusing fish value with cooked-food value

A cooked stack selling for 8g is not an 8g profit if the raw fish would have sold for 5g. Your actual conversion profit may be only 2g after spices and AH costs.

This is the most common mistake in fishing guides.

## 2. Fishing the right fish at the wrong time

Fishing Nightfin during the day, Sunscale at night, or Winter Squid outside its profitable season can turn a premium route into mediocre vendor income.

Use the clock and the AH together.

## 3. Treating theoretical gold/hour as real gold/hour

A guide may report the value of a full hour of uninterrupted pool fishing. You may spend that hour:

- traveling,
- searching for pools,
- relisting auctions,
- getting killed,
- waiting for respawns,
- standing at the mailbox,
- cooking fish that do not sell.

Your real metric is:

\[
\text{Real gold/hour}
=
\frac{\text{sale proceeds} - \text{costs}}{\text{total time logged in}}
\]

not the value of fish caught during the casts that actually landed.
